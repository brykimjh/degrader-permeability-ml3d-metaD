An alternative approach for generating initial 3D conformations is to use MOE (Molecular Operating Environment). The SMILES strings can first be imported into the MOE database, where they are stored and visualized in the Database Viewer. After selecting the desired entries, the Compute → QuickPrep function can be applied to automatically generate 3D coordinates, assign protonation states, and perform a basic structural cleanup. Each resulting structure can then be exported individually as a PDB file, providing a reasonable starting geometry for subsequent force field parameterization and further refinement.
